Mail Tutor

An AI engineering tutor that lives entirely in your inbox.

Proof of concept

The whole course happens on one email thread

  1. StudentWrites inAny email to the tutor starts the course.
  2. TutorSends an assignmentA story, a goal and links to two help pages.
  3. StudentReplies with a zipBuilt with an AI coding tool of their choice.
  4. TutorGrades itHidden tests decide. The next level follows a pass.

No app, no login, no website to learn.

Saying hello is the sign-up

Anyone who writes in becomes a student. There is no sign-up page.

Every email carries the same blue status box and progress bar.

The first assignment asks students to try alone

A second email brings the story, the Level 1 goal and a short how-to-submit note.

It links to an Assignment page and a Hints and help page.

Design choice: the ready-made prompt lives on the hints page, not in the email.

A zip goes out, a verdict comes back

The student replies on the same thread, using Claude Code, GitHub Copilot or any tool.

The zip runs in a sandbox against hidden tickets. The reply opens with Pass, Fail or Not graded, then a short LLM-written note.

A failure teaches without giving the answer

The student stays on the same level and keeps the thread.

The note names a likely cause. The result line shows the score: 1 of 5 here, with 4 needed.

Hint 1 points to the Hints and help page. A second fail gives a stronger hint.

Triage tickets is a ladder of three levels

classify.zip

1. Classify

Read one support ticket and tag it bug, billing, access or other.

route.zip

2. Route

Send the ticket to the right team and give it a priority.

reply.zip

3. Reply

Draft a short customer reply, checked by a custom checker.

A level is passed at 4 of 5 hidden tickets right.

Each pass carries the next level in the same reply

Level 2 passed. Tries left today drops by one with each graded submission.

Level 3 passed. All three bars are full.

The ladder has a proper ending

A separate celebration email suggests what to do next.

Write again after finishing and the card is already marked finished. A new zip gets a short note, with no grading and no tries used.

Every level has two generated pages

Hidden tickets

The room

AgentCore runs the student's main.py with no internet, one ticket at a time. It sees ticket inputs only.

input 1 → output input 2 → output input 3 → output input 4 → output input 5 → output

Checker

Trusted code in the Lambda scores each output.

1 right2 right3 wrong4 right5 right

Example: 4 of 5, Pass
