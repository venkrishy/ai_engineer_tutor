Triage tickets · Level 3 of 3 (Reply) · Assignment

Draft the first reply to the customer

Hints and help

Your triage program now sorts and routes every ticket. The last step is the customer's experience: a ticket nobody answers feels ignored. Extend the program so it also drafts a short, friendly first reply that tells the customer which team has their ticket.

Level 3 goal: extend your program so it also prints a short customer reply that contains the ticket id, stays under 80 words, and says "refund" only when the ticket does.

What your program reads and prints

Your program is main.py. The grader runs it once per ticket with python3 main.py, sends the ticket as JSON on standard input, and reads standard output. Print exactly one JSON object and nothing else.

Input, example 1
{
  "id": "S-1",
  "subject": "Refund for a cancelled order",
  "body": "I cancelled my order last week but the payment is still on my card."
}
Output, example 1
{
  "id": "S-1",
  "label": "billing",
  "priority": "P2",
  "queue": "billing",
  "reply": "Hi, thanks for contacting us about S-1. Our billing team is looking into your refund and will follow up soon."
}
Input, example 2
{
  "id": "S-2",
  "subject": "Password reset email never arrives",
  "body": "I asked for a new password three times and nothing shows up."
}
Output, example 2
{
  "id": "S-2",
  "label": "access",
  "priority": "P2",
  "queue": "support",
  "reply": "Hi, thanks for contacting us about S-2. Our support team is looking into your password reset and will follow up soon."
}
Input, example 3
{
  "id": "S-3",
  "subject": "App crashes when I upload a photo",
  "body": "The page shows an error and the whole site is down for me."
}
Output, example 3
{
  "id": "S-3",
  "label": "bug",
  "priority": "P1",
  "queue": "engineering",
  "reply": "Hi, thanks for contacting us about S-3. Our engineering team is looking into the crash and will follow up soon."
}
Input, example 4
{
  "id": "S-4",
  "subject": "How do I export my data?",
  "body": "I would like a copy of my notes."
}
Output, example 4
{
  "id": "S-4",
  "label": "other",
  "priority": "P3",
  "queue": "support",
  "reply": "Hi, thanks for contacting us about S-4. Our support team will send you the steps to export your data soon."
}

These are examples. The grader checks your program on five other tickets you have not seen, so your program must follow the rules, not remember the examples. Your reply wording can differ from the example; the checker tests its rules.

The rules

The label. Join the subject and the body, make it lowercase, and look for these words:

  • billing: charge, charged, invoice, refund, payment, billed, billing, receipt
  • access: login, log in, sign in, password, permission, locked out, access
  • bug: broken, crash, error, not working, fails, wrong, bug

If more than one group matches, billing wins over access, and access wins over bug. If no group matches, the label is other.

The queue. A bug goes to engineering, billing goes to billing, and everything else (access and other) goes to support.

The priority. Look at the lowercased subject and body and check in this order:

  • P1 (work is blocked or money is wrong): cannot, can't, unable, locked out, down, outage, twice, duplicate, overcharged, wrong amount
  • P3 (a how-to question): how do i, how can i, how to, where is, where can i, where do i
  • P2 is everything else

The reply. Build a short, friendly message for the customer from a template:

  • It must contain the ticket id, for example S-1.
  • It must be under 80 words. Aim for 25 to 45.
  • It says which team (engineering, billing, or support) is looking at it.
  • Use the word "refund" only when the customer's own subject or body contains it.
  • Plain text, polite, no emojis.

How to test it

Run one of the examples from a terminal in the folder that holds main.py:

echo '{"id": "S-1", "subject": "Refund for a cancelled order", "body": "I cancelled my order last week but the payment is still on my card."}' | python3 main.py

You should see:

{"id": "S-1", "label": "billing", "priority": "P2", "queue": "billing", "reply": "Hi, thanks for contacting us about S-1. Our billing team is looking into your refund and will follow up soon."}

What to send back

Zip your program and reply to the tutor email with the zip attached. One zip per email, 10 MB or less. The grader needs main.py at the top of the zip, or inside a single folder.

reply.zip

Also fine, with one folder inside:

reply.zip
└── reply/
    └── main.py

On a Mac or Linux, zip reply.zip main.py makes it.
